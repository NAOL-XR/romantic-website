Our Love Story ❤️

A story written in small moments.

Scene 1 — The First Interest

I went to the library.

She came with her friend and passed in front of me.

I didn't know it then... but that was where my story began.

Scene 2 — The Stationery

After the first moment, I kept thinking about her hair all day.

Later, I went to the stationery place and waited for some paper.

Then she came... and stood right beside me.

I became nervous. ❤️

Scene 3 — The Room 🚪

I went to the study space on the first floor.

I found a room and opened the door...

She was there.

I suddenly became nervous and walked out of the room.

I wasn't ready for that moment. ❤️

Scene 4 — Church & University ⛪🎓

For three or four days, I didn't see her.

I went to church to talk to God and find peace within myself.

But honestly... I missed her.

On the way back to the university, I told my friend:

"God, you know... just let me see her eyes once."

And then... she was there.

We looked at each other, and my friend and I were excited.

Scene 5 — Birthday 🎂

It was my birthday.

I didn't know she would be there... but somehow, I felt she might be.

My friend and I went to the building.

She was studying on the first floor.

I went to the second floor. I prepared myself to finally talk to her.

But when I came back... she had already left.

I was ready this time. But the moment was gone.

Scene 6 — After the Moment 🌙

After that day, I went back to my normal life.

But sometimes, in the quiet moments, I still thought about her.

I wondered where she was... what she was doing... and whether she ever thought about me.

I didn't know her feelings. I only knew mine.

And that question stayed with me.

Scene 7 — The Memories ✨

Sometimes, one small moment can stay in your mind for a long time.

The library... the stationery place... the room... the church... the university... my birthday.

Different places. Different moments. But somehow, they all reminded me of her.

I still didn't know what she felt.

And maybe that was the hardest part...

I kept wondering.

Scene 8 — The Question ❤️

Maybe I was tired of wondering.

So I finally asked...

Would you go on a date with me?

Scene 9 — Choose the Time ⏱️

When should we meet?

Scene 10 ❤️

Thank you for saying yes.

✦ I'll see you then. The End ✦

Some stories are not planned.
They simply begin with one small moment.
